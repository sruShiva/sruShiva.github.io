01 / RESEARCH

Research and projects.

What I'm working on now, and some things I've built.

CURRENT RESEARCHJan 2026 — Present

Intrusion detection and shortcut learning

At the NextGArch Lab, I'm testing whether packet-payload features can improve intrusion detection over rule-based systems like Snort, using the CIC-IDS2017 dataset. Feature-importance analysis and decision-rule extraction showed that the models were relying on shortcuts, and I'm helping develop ways to prevent that.

Machine learningNetworkingSecurity
CURRENT RESEARCHApr 2026 — Present

Intersectional bias in LLM-generated personas

With Blablablab, I study the “ideal candidate” personas that LLMs write from job descriptions. Models evaluate both their own personas and other models’, and I look at where they agree, where they disagree, and which bias patterns keep coming back.

LLM evaluationReproducibilityBias
SYSTEMS PROJECTFall 2025

FastSim: incremental GPU-accelerated RTL simulation

A Python/CUDA prototype that recompiles only the Verilog modules that changed, using content hashes and a cache of compiled CUDA kernels. It has a Verilog parser, a Verilog-to-CUDA transpiler and a command-line tool, and I tested it on small circuits (AND, ALU, counter). It is still a prototype.

CUDACompilersVerilog
SYSTEMS PROJECTSpring 2025

CUDA image processing

CUDA kernels for Gaussian blur, grayscale conversion and Sobel-style edge detection. Making the blur separable cuts the work per pixel from O(r²) to O(2r). On one test image it ran 10.0× faster than OpenCV on the CPU (1.30 ms vs. 13.06 ms).

GPU programmingPerformanceImage processing
TEAM PROJECTFall 2022 — Spring 2023

Wearable music gloves for neurorehabilitation

We designed gloves to help with hand dexterity after a stroke, with interactive exercises, music-based feedback and progress tracking. Patent application 202421008736.
