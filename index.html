With Blablablab, I design cross-model evaluations of LLM-generated candidate personas, investigating how overlapping attributes shape generated content, model judgments, and the reliability of their explanations.

SYSTEMS PROJECTFall 2025

FastSim — incremental GPU-accelerated RTL simulation

Prototyped a Python/CUDA workflow for compiling changed Verilog modules using content hashes and cached kernels. Explored ways to reduce the compile–test iteration cycle through selective recompilation.

SYSTEMS PROJECTSpring 2025

CUDA-accelerated image processing

Implemented GPU kernels for Gaussian blur, grayscale conversion, and edge detection, studying decomposition, thread-level parallelism, and memory access. Measured a 10× blur speedup in a project test compared with an OpenCV CPU baseline.
