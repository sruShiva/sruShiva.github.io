01 / SYSTEMSTHOUGHT

The most beautiful part of systems

I think the most beautiful part of systems comes down to four questions:

  1. How beautifully do you formulate the problem?
  2. How well do you balance accuracy and latency?
  3. How well do you balance memory and hardware constraints?
  4. How well do you generalize, and how adaptive is the solution?

If you’re answering these four questions, you can fit a lot of new solutions.
