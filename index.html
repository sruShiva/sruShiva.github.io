01 / RESEARCH

Research & selected work.

Problems I've explored across systems and machine learning.

CURRENT RESEARCHJan 2026 — Present

Learning for reliable intrusion detection

At the NextGArch Lab, I study whether learned detectors use attack-relevant payload characteristics or rely on dataset artifacts. I benchmark payload-only features against Snort on identical CIC-IDS2017 test packets, and use feature-importance analysis and decision-rule extraction to identify shortcut learning.

Machine learningSystems evaluationRobustness
CURRENT RESEARCHApr 2026 — Present

Intersectional bias in language models

With Blablablab, I design cross-model evaluations of LLM-generated candidate personas, investigating how overlapping attributes shape generated content, model judgments, and the reliability of their explanations.

LLM evaluationReproducibilityResponsible AI
SYSTEMS PROJECTFall 2025

FastSim — incremental GPU-accelerated RTL simulation

Prototyped a Python/CUDA workflow for compiling changed Verilog modules using content hashes and cached kernels. Explored ways to reduce the compile–test iteration cycle through selective recompilation.

CUDACompilersIncremental computation
SYSTEMS PROJECTSpring 2025

CUDA-accelerated image processing

Implemented GPU kernels for Gaussian blur, grayscale conversion, and edge detection, using a separable blur to cut per-pixel work from O(r²) to O(2r), and studying thread-level parallelism and memory access. Measured a 10.0× blur speedup over an OpenCV CPU baseline (13.06 ms vs. 1.30 ms) on a single test image.

GPU programmingPerformanceImage processing
TEAM PROJECTFall 2022 — Spring 2023

Wearable music gloves for neurorehabilitation

Co-designed, in a team of three, wearable music gloves to support post-stroke hand dexterity, with interactive exercises, music-based feedback, and progress tracking. Patent application 202421008736.
