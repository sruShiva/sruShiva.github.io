Research and projects

Intrusion detection and shortcut learning

Jan 2026 – present · NextGArch Lab, University of Michigan

I'm testing whether packet-payload features can improve intrusion detection over rule-based systems like Snort, using the CIC-IDS2017 dataset. I engineered payload-only features and compared them with Snort on identical test packets. Feature-importance analysis and decision-rule extraction showed that the models were relying on shortcuts, and I'm helping develop ways to prevent that.

Intersectional bias in LLM-generated personas

Apr 2026 – present · Blablablab, University of Michigan

I study intersectional bias in “ideal candidate” personas that LLMs write from job descriptions. In my experiments, models evaluate both their own personas and those of other models, and I look at where they agree, where they disagree, and which bias patterns keep coming back.

FastSim: incremental GPU-accelerated RTL simulation

Fall 2025

A Python/CUDA prototype that recompiles only the Verilog modules that changed, using content hashes and a cache of compiled CUDA kernels. It includes a Verilog parser, a Verilog-to-CUDA transpiler and a command-line tool, and I tested it on small circuits (AND, ALU, counter). It is still a prototype.

CUDA image processing

Spring 2025

CUDA kernels for Gaussian blur, grayscale conversion and Sobel-style edge detection. Making the blur separable cuts the work per pixel from O(r²) to O(2r). On one test image it ran 10.0× faster than OpenCV on the CPU (1.30 ms vs. 13.06 ms).

Wearable music gloves for neurorehabilitation

Fall 2022 – Spring 2023 · Team of three

We designed gloves to help with hand dexterity after a stroke, with interactive exercises, music-based feedback and progress tracking. Patent application 202421008736.
